      Pricing questions

      Before you pay.

      Is the estimate the final price?

      It's our honest price for what you've chosen. Before we cut, we confirm measurements (in person or from your photos). If anything changes — size, timber, extras — we tell you first and settle the difference in the balance.

      What's included?

      Design and drawings, timber, joinery, finishing and hardware. Delivery is calculated from our workshop to your state; installation is optional and shown separately.

      How long is my invoice valid?

      Invoices are valid for 14 days, because timber prices move. After that, create a fresh one in a minute.

      How do I pay?

      By bank transfer. Your personalised invoice shows the account details, the exact amount and a reference. After paying, tap I've paid on the invoice to send us your receipt on WhatsApp.
