Refectory Table

About this piece

The top is glued up from boards chosen and arranged so the grain flows from end to end. The underframe is mortised and tenoned, then draw-bored with hardwood pegs — no screws or metal brackets doing the real work.

We finish it in a hard-wax oil that you can spot-repair at home: a scratch or a hot-mug ring can be sanded locally and re-oiled without refinishing the whole top.

Make it yours
