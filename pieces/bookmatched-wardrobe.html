About this piece

Bookmatching means slicing a board and opening it out so each door is the mirror of the other. It takes careful selection, and it is what makes a plain cupboard look like a piece of furniture.

Inside we fit hanging rails, shelves and drawers to your wardrobe — tell us how many shirts, dresses and shoes need a home and we will plan the layout.

Make it yours

  • Freestanding or fitted wall-to-wall
  • Mirror on the inside of a door
  • Internal drawers and shoe racks
  • Lighting that switches on with the door

Sizes, timber and finish are all made to order. Delivery across our delivery area.
