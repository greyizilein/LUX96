Ask a question
How long does a commission take?

Most pieces take 4–8 weeks from the day you approve the drawing to delivery. Large fitted jobs such as kitchens and full wardrobes can take longer; we give you a date before we start and keep you updated with photos along the way.

How do payments work?

We take a 60% deposit to buy timber and start the build. The balance is due when the piece is finished and ready for delivery — you can inspect it (or ask for photos and a video) before you pay. Build your project on the pricing page for an instant invoice with payment details.

Can I choose the size, timber and finish?

Yes, everything is made to order. Every piece on this site is a starting point — tell us your measurements, choose from the timber rack, and pick a finish (natural oil, hard-wax oil, lacquer or stain).

Do you deliver and install?

Yes. We deliver ourselves or with a trusted carrier, and our team installs fitted work like wardrobes, kitchens, doors and panelling. See the delivery map for coverage.

What does the guarantee cover?

Our joinery is guaranteed for 10 years: if a joint we cut fails in normal use, we repair it. Normal wear, water damage, direct sun and misuse aren't covered — the care guide shows how to keep your piece at its best.

Can you match an existing piece or a photo?

Often, yes. Send us photos with rough measurements on WhatsApp. We'll tell you honestly what we can match, and suggest timber and finish to sit well with what you already own.

Do you restore old furniture?

Yes — re-gluing loose joints, repairing breaks, refinishing tired surfaces and re-upholstery. Send photos and we'll advise whether it's worth restoring.

Do you work with interior designers and businesses?

Yes. We build to drawings and specifications for designers, offices, restaurants and hotels, and can supply samples for approval before production.

How a commission works

  1. I

    Talk

    Tell us what you need — a sketch, a photo, a measurement. In person, by phone or on WhatsApp.

  2. II

    Draw & quote

    We measure up, draw the piece, bring timber samples and give you a clear quote and timeline.

  3. III

    Build

    Boards are selected, jointed and finished in our workshop, with progress photos as it comes together.

  4. IV

    Install

    We deliver, assemble and fit — and don't leave until it is exactly right.
