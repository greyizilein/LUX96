01 — The Studio

LUX96 is a woodworking studio for people who would rather buy once. We draw every piece around the room it will live in, choose the boards ourselves, and join them so they stay joined — no flat-pack, no filler, no shortcuts you will find in ten years' time.

How a commission works

  1. I

    Talk

    Tell us what you need — a sketch, a photo, a measurement. In person, by phone or on WhatsApp.

  2. II

    Draw & quote

    We measure up, draw the piece, bring timber samples and give you a clear quote and timeline.

  3. III

    Build

    Boards are selected, jointed and finished in our workshop, with progress photos as it comes together.

  4. IV

    Install

    We deliver, assemble and fit — and don't leave until it is exactly right.
