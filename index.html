The joint is the promise

Held by wood,
not by luck.

Screws loosen and glue lines fail. Interlocking joints get tighter under load. We cut dovetails, mortise & tenons and bridle joints so your piece survives moves, children and decades.

  • Dovetail drawers & carcasses
  • Mortise & tenon tables & frames
  • Bridle legs & rails
  • Tongue & groove panels & tops
