Our story

Wood, worked with care.

LUX96 Furnitures is a woodworking studio that believes good furniture should be built once and loved for a lifetime. Every piece starts as a conversation and ends as something solid, beautiful and uniquely yours.

We combine traditional joinery with modern tools and finishes, working closely with homeowners, interior designers, offices and hospitality spaces to bring ideas to life — from a single bedside table to a fully fitted home.

  • Quality timber, chosen and seasoned for stability
  • Durable joinery and hand-applied finishes
  • Honest pricing and clear timelines
  • Delivery and professional installation

How it works

From idea to installation

  1. 01

    Consult

    Tell us what you need. We discuss your space, style, budget and timeline — in person, by phone or on WhatsApp.

  2. 02

    Design & Quote

    We take measurements, prepare drawings and recommend materials and finishes, then give you a clear quote.

  3. 03

    Craft

    Your piece is built in our workshop by skilled hands, with progress updates along the way.

  4. 04

    Deliver & Install

    We deliver, assemble and install — and make sure everything is exactly right before we leave.
